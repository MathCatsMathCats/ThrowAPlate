Privacy

The short version: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no accounts of its own, and its ads are personalised only if you agree.

The appThis website

The Throw a Plate at the Wall!!! app

The app doesn't ask for your name, email address or location, and we run no servers that receive data from it.

  • Ads: Google AdMob shows a banner, occasional full-screen ads and optional rewarded ads. Personalised ads only with your consent.
  • Camera, photos and microphone: only for features you use (your wall photo, AR Mode, Yell Boost). Everything stays on your iPhone, and nothing is recorded.
  • Progress: stored on your iPhone only. We can't see it.
  • Game Center: optional. If you're signed in, Apple handles your leaderboard scores.

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Every page, picture and script comes from this website itself; nothing is loaded from other companies. Links to other sites are plain links, and nothing is loaded from them until you click.

The mini game on the home page runs only in your browser. Its sounds are generated there, and nothing you do in it is saved or sent anywhere.
